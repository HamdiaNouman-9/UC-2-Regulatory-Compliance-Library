                            SECP Holds Talk Series with Yousef Hassan Khalawi

                            SECP holds Talk Series with Yousef Hassan Khalawi, Secretary General of ICCD, highlighting Pakistan’s potential to expand its role in the global Islamic economy through innovative Shariah-compliant finance, fintech and stronger professional expertise. The session explored opportunities in Waqf, agricultural finance and Qard Hassan, emphasizing inclusive and sustainable economic development.
