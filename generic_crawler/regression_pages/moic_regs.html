Industrial Areas

The Industrial Areas Law in the Kingdom of Bahrain is concerned with all matters related to the establishment and organization of industrial zones, the exploitation of the lands of industrial zones and construction in the industrial zones, the obligations and rights of the lessee for industrial lands and penalties.
